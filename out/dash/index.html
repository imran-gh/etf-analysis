Method and caveats
  • Universe: every ETF on Nasdaq Trader's list of US-traded securities, all exchanges, international funds included. Prices are Yahoo Finance closes adjusted for dividends and splits.
  • Leveraged and inverse funds were dropped by name. Funds with stale prices or bad data spikes were dropped.
  • Return is yearly growth (CAGR). Volatility is the annualised standard deviation of daily returns. Sharpe uses T-bill fund BIL as the risk-free rate.
  • "Luck" test: each fund's monthly lead over SPY, with a Newey-West t-test and Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all funds.
  • Survivorship bias: closed ETFs are not in today's list, so results look better than reality.
  • Mixes were searched over 40 funds (the strongest 2011–2019 funds plus common diversifiers) in 10–20% weight steps.
